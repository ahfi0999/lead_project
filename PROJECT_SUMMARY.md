# Project Summary: Lead Conversion Predictor

**Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Project Status:** ✅ Complete and Ready for Deployment

---

## What Was Built

An AI-powered web application that predicts which coaching leads are most likely to convert into paying customers. The system analyzes 8 different factors about each lead and provides:

- **Conversion Probability Score** (0-100%)
- **Automatic Lead Classification** (HOT/WARM/COLD)
- **Recommended Actions** for sales team

---

## Key Numbers

- **9,240+** historical lead records analyzed
- **8** input factors considered per lead
- **3** lead categories (HOT/WARM/COLD)
- **100%** complete and documented

---

## Business Benefits

1. **Better Prioritization** - Focus on leads most likely to convert
2. **Time Savings** - Automated lead qualification
3. **Higher Conversion** - Contact high-value leads immediately
4. **Cost Efficiency** - Reduce time spent on low-probability leads

---

## How It Works

```
Input Lead Info → AI Model Analyzes → Get Score & Recommendation
```

**Example Results:**
- Lead scores 85% → 🔥 **HOT LEAD** → "Call immediately"
- Lead scores 65% → ⚠️ **WARM LEAD** → "Send email follow-up"
- Lead scores 30% → ❄️ **COLD LEAD** → "Add to nurturing list"
